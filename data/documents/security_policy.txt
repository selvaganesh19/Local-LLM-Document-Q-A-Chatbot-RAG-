ACME CORPORATION - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document SEC-2026-04 - Effective 1 February 2026

1. SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns and third parties who
access Acme systems, networks or data, on any device, from any location.

2. DATA CLASSIFICATION

All information must be assigned one of four classifications:

  - Public: may be shared freely outside Acme.
  - Internal: may be shared within Acme; disclosure requires approval.
  - Confidential: restricted to named individuals with a business need.
  - Restricted: the highest classification, applying to personal data of
    customers, payment credentials and authentication secrets.

Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in
transit using TLS 1.3 or later.

3. AUTHENTICATION

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days. Reuse
of any of the previous 12 passwords is prohibited. Multi-factor authentication
is mandatory for all remote access, for all administrative accounts, and for any
access to Restricted data, without exception.

Shared accounts are prohibited. Service accounts must be owned by a named
individual, documented in the service register, and reviewed every quarter.

4. DEVICE SECURITY

All laptops and mobile devices used for Acme work must have full disk encryption
enabled and an approved endpoint protection agent installed. Devices must be
patched within 14 days of a critical security update being released, and within
30 days for high severity updates.

Removable USB storage is prohibited for Confidential and Restricted data.
Where transfer is unavoidable, use the approved managed file transfer service.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING

Any suspected or confirmed security incident must be reported to the Security
team at security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery. Incidents affecting
Restricted data must additionally be escalated to the Data Protection Officer
immediately.

Incident responders must preserve evidence and must not attempt independent
remediation of a suspected compromise of an administrative account.

6. REGULATORY NOTIFICATION

Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to data subjects,
Acme must notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of
the breach. Affected data subjects must be notified without undue delay where
the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

7. ACCESS CONTROL

Access is granted on the principle of least privilege. Access rights are
reviewed every quarter by system owners. Access must be revoked within 24 hours
of an employee leaving Acme, and within 4 hours for anyone holding
administrative privileges.

8. THIRD PARTIES

Third parties with access to Acme data must have a signed data processing
agreement in place before any access is granted. Third-party access entitlements
are reviewed quarterly. Any third party processing Restricted data must provide
evidence of an independent security assessment no older than 12 months.

9. BACKUPS AND RECOVERY

Production data is backed up daily with a 35 day retention window. Backups must
be encrypted to the same standard as the source data. Restoration tests are
performed every 6 months and the results recorded in the resilience register.

10. SECURITY TRAINING

All staff complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining and
annually thereafter. Staff who handle Restricted data complete additional
specialist training every 12 months. Failure to complete mandatory training
within 60 days results in suspension of access to Confidential and Restricted
systems.

11. VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT

Authenticated vulnerability scans run weekly on all internet-facing systems and
monthly on internal systems. Critical vulnerabilities must be remediated within
14 days of identification; high severity within 30 days. Exceptions require a
documented risk acceptance approved by the Chief Information Security Officer.

12. ACCEPTABLE USE

Acme systems may not be used for cryptocurrency mining, for hosting personal
services, or for any activity that breaches export control regulations. Personal
use of email and internet access is tolerated where it is reasonable in volume
and does not interfere with duties.

13. LOGGING AND MONITORING

Authentication events, administrative actions and access to Restricted data are
logged centrally and retained for 12 months. Logs must be protected against
modification and reviewed for anomalies at least weekly.
