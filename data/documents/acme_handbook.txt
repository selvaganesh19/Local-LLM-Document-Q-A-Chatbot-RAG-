ACME CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 4.2 - Effective 1 January 2026

1. WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:30 local time, Monday to Friday, with a
45 minute unpaid lunch break. This gives a contractual 37.5 hour week. Teams may
agree a shifted schedule with their manager provided core collaboration hours of
10:00 to 15:00 are preserved.

2. REMOTE WORK

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote days must be
recorded in the workforce system by 17:00 on the preceding working day. Fully
remote arrangements require director approval and are reviewed every six months.
Employees working remotely must be reachable on the corporate messaging platform
during core hours.

3. PROBATIONARY PERIOD

All new employees serve a probationary period of six months. During probation the
notice period is two weeks on either side. Probation may be extended once, by up
to three months, where performance objectives have not yet been demonstrated.

4. NOTICE PERIODS

After successful completion of probation the notice periods are:

  - Employees in grades 1 to 4: 30 calendar days.
  - Employees in grade 5 and above: 60 calendar days.
  - Senior staff holding the title of Head of Department or above: 90 calendar
    days.

Notice must be given in writing to the line manager and to the People team.
Acme may pay salary in lieu of all or part of the notice period.

5. ANNUAL LEAVE

The annual leave entitlement is 25 days per calendar year plus 8 public
holidays. Leave accrues monthly at 2.08 days. A maximum of 5 unused days may be
carried into the following year and must be taken before 31 March or they are
forfeited. Requests for leave of more than 10 consecutive working days require
manager approval at least 30 days in advance.

6. SICK LEAVE

Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
Absences longer than seven calendar days require a fit note. Sick pay is paid at
full salary for the first 20 working days in any rolling 12 month period, and at
half salary for the following 20 working days.

7. EXPENSES

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Claims older than 60 days will not be reimbursed. Receipts are required for any
single item over 15 euros. Mileage is reimbursed at 0.42 euros per kilometre.

8. PENSION

Acme matches employee pension contributions up to 6 percent of base salary. The
match vests immediately. Employees are auto-enrolled after three months of
service and may opt out at any time.

9. PARENTAL LEAVE

Primary carers are entitled to 20 weeks of leave at full pay. Secondary carers
are entitled to 6 weeks of leave at full pay. Both entitlements may be taken in
up to three separate blocks within 12 months of birth or placement.

10. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 euros. Requests for courses
costing more than 500 euros require manager approval. Study leave of up to five
days per year is available for approved professional examinations.

11. EQUIPMENT

Acme provides a laptop, monitor and headset for every employee. Equipment
remains the property of Acme and must be returned within 14 days of the last
working day. Loss or damage must be reported to the IT service desk within 2
working days.

12. DISCIPLINARY PROCESS

The disciplinary process has three stages: a verbal warning, a written warning,
and a final written warning. Warnings remain on file for 12 months. Gross
misconduct may result in summary dismissal without notice.
